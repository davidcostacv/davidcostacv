# Hi, I'm David 👋

I'm a student learning software development by building things I actually use: mobile apps, music tools and small bots.
Lately I build most of it with AI in the loop, pairing with AI coding agents to go from idea to working app quickly.

- 🎓 Learning: C, C++, Python and web fundamentals
- 🛠️ Building: mobile apps with Expo, Python tools and automations
- 🤖 Interested in: practical AI, meaning tools that save real time every day
- 🎧 Side passion: music and DJing

## 🚀 Featured projects

### [dj-set-builder](https://github.com/davidcostacv/dj-set-builder)
Reads my Spotify library and builds harmonically mixed DJ sets ordered by BPM, key and energy arc, then creates the playlist straight in Spotify.
`Python` · `SQLite` · `Spotify API` · `GetSongBPM`

### Llavero *(private, in development)*
One app with lots of small everyday tools: gym tracker, calories, habits, subscriptions, expenses, a document vault, PDF tools, notes and focus timer. Everything is stored on the phone and works offline.
`TypeScript` · `Expo` · `React Native` · `SQLite` · iOS & Android

### [Market-bot](https://github.com/davidcostacv/Market-bot)
A collection of bots and automations:
- **Market Pulse**: a scheduled scan that turns market news into a scored, deduplicated feed with a live dashboard, and only notifies when something really matters.
- **WhatsApp Macro Bot**: text it what you ate and it replies with the macros and your running total for the day.

`Python` · `Node.js` · `Telegram` · `WhatsApp`
